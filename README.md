## 🚀 What It Does
* **Unblocks Gaming & Streaming:** Fully unblocks Steam (Store, Community, Downloads), Twitch (Live Streams, Video, Chat), Discord, Telegram, and restricted websites.
* **100% Full Speed (No VPN Ping):** Unlike VPNs, your internet speed and gaming ping remain at their absolute fastest.
* **Encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH):** Encrypts all DNS traffic so the ISP cannot intercept, inspect, or tamper with your requests.
* **Completely Portable:** No installation required, zero RAM usage when closed (~7 KB binary).

## 📖 How to Use

### 1. To Enable Unblocking:
1. Double-click `Enable_Secure_DNS.exe`.
2. Click **Yes** when Windows prompts for Administrator permission (UAC).
3. Once you see `[SUCCESS]`, your entire PC is now secured and unblocked!
4. *(Optional)* Press `[G]` to open the GitHub repository.

### 2. To Disable / Restore Default Settings:
1. Double-click `Disable_Secure_DNS.exe`.
2. Click **Yes** on the Administrator prompt.
3. Your network settings and DNS will instantly revert back to automatic (DHCP).
