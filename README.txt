What this tool does:
- Unblocks Steam (Store, Community, Downloads), Twitch (Streams & Chat),
  Discord, Telegram, and restricted websites on YemenNet.
- Encrypts all DNS queries over HTTPS (DoH) so the ISP cannot block them.
- Keeps your FULL internet speed and lowest gaming ping (NO VPN lag).
- Lightweight (~7 KB) and portable with zero background memory usage.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE:
----------------------------------------------------------------------

[1] TO ENABLE UNBLOCKING:
    - Double-click "Enable_Secure_DNS.exe".
    - Click "Yes" when Windows asks for Administrator permission.
    - Press any key when you see [SUCCESS] (or press 'G' to visit GitHub).
    - Done! Your entire PC is now secured and unblocked.

[2] TO RESTORE DEFAULT SETTINGS:
    - Double-click "Disable_Secure_DNS.exe".
    - Click "Yes" on the Administrator prompt.
    - Done! Your network is restored to automatic (DHCP).
